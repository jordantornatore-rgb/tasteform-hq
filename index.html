  2. Name it Meta Ads and paste https://mcp.facebook.com/ads as the server URL.
  3. Log in with Facebook, pick the TasteForm ad account, and choose read-only access.
  4. Tell Claude it's connected. The daily ads sync then switches on and this tab fills by the next morning.
