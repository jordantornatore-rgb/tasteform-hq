# Content Engine tab

The **Content Engine** tab (sidebar → Growth, phone → More → Content Engine, hash `#content`) is the front end for the creator **Scanner** (research), Jordan's **script editor**, the Mac Mini **autopost pipeline** (edit → trial reels → posting through Outstand) and the Sunday **review**. Everyone signed in can read it; only the `admin` profile can change anything. Every direct write is a merge into the row's `data` (the `db.doc().update()` path in `index.html`); script saves, moves and post edits go through the `content-update` / `content-reschedule` functions.

**Workflow v2 (4 Nov 2026): Jordan writes his own scripts.** The system gives him ideas, hooks, visual hooks and talking points; he writes the words in the script editor, films two weeks at a time from the filming sheet, and approves edits while auto post is off. Nothing in the tab or the pipeline writes his `script`.

All dates are Sydney days (`HQ.sydNow`, `HQ.weekStart`, `HQ.fmtDay` from `hq-core.js`). Numbers are exact or `–` with the reason next to them; nothing is estimated.

### Script editor (Turn into a reel, any open slot, + New reel, "Edit script" on a post)
Full screen on a phone, a large side panel on desktop. Title; up to 5 spoken hooks (the idea's hooks, and the old AI hooks on an open slot, are tap-to-use chips, never prefilled); visual hook (the idea's `visualHooks` as chips); the script (auto-growing, keeps line breaks, word count and read time at 2.5 words a second); editing notes; CTA / ending line; format, funnel, location; for a new reel a slot picker listing the open slots of the next 3 weeks or a new day + slot type; the source idea panel (original reel, numbers, similar reels, talking points). The old AI-drafted `body[]` beats show read-only as reference. A local draft autosaves every 3 seconds (`localStorage` `hqScriptDraft:<id>`, wrapped in try/catch) and is offered back when the editor reopens. **Save script** calls `content-update` with `{id, create?:{date, slot}, fields:{title, hooks, visualHook, script, editNotes, cta, format, funnel, location, sourceIdea}}`; a new reel gets `id = <weekStart>-<day>-<hi|lo|car>-<4 random chars>`. The function creates the row, flips `needs_script` to `to_film` once there's a script, and marks the idea planned.

### Add a filmed reel ("+ Add filmed reel" in the Library, "Filmed this? Add the footage" on a to-film reel)
A side panel (full screen on a phone): pick an open slot (needs script / to film, a week back to 4 weeks ahead) or a new reel on a day + slot type, a title, **Raw footage** (the pipeline edits it, makes 5 versions, trials them) or **Finished edit** (one file: posted as it is after your approval, no trial; 2 or more files: versions v1…vN, sorted by file name, trialled like any reel), and where it is: **Upload from this device** (several files; videos, photos, audio; resumable TUS upload in 6 MB chunks to the private bucket `content-footage/<rowId>/`, files over 45 MB go up as 45 MB parts because the plan caps one stored object at 50 MB, joined again on the Mac; progress per file, keep the screen open) or **Already on the Mac** (a folder in `footage/`, from `meta autopost.footageFolders`). Optional format, funnel, location, what you said, editing notes. It creates the row through `content-update` when needed, then merges `footageUpload {source: upload|mac, kind: raw|final, status: uploading|uploaded|linked, files[{name, path, size, type, url, urlExpires}], folder}` and moves `needs_script` to `to_film`. After the upload HQ creates a 7-day signed download link per file; opening the Library refreshes links that expire within 2 days until the Mac has pulled them (`footageUpload.pulledAt`). Cards show "Uploading n%", "Footage uploaded · on the Mac within 20 min", "Footage links expired" or "Footage problem"; the post sheet shows the footage block.

### Filming sheet
Every `to_film` row for the next 2 weeks, grouped by location: day, slot, format, title, the hooks numbered, the visual hook, the script and the ending line in big type, editing notes. Print button (print CSS shows only the sheet).

### Phone preview (▶ on any card or "Phone preview" in the post sheet)
A 9:16 frame playing the final file (`<video controls playsinline preload="metadata">`): `video.previewUrl`, a version's `mediaUrl`, or a 1-hour signed URL from `content-previews` when only `video.storagePath` is set (renewed when the sheet reopens). Username row, the caption clamped to two lines with "…more", posting time and platforms, the sound line ("♪ title · 10% under voice"; music is mixed into the file), the versions v1–v5 as tabs with "what's different" and live trial numbers, 🏆 on the winner with its why and low-confidence chip, Approve / Request changes while waiting. "Not edited yet" before a render; a video that fails to load shows "Preview didn't load – the pipeline re-uploads on its next run" and merges `video.previewBroken:true`.

### Post sheet
Preview + Phone preview, Needs-you reason and Mark fixed, posting error, "The video was cut from the old script" + Re-edit, ⭐ gold standard (approved, trialing, scheduled or posted), YouTube claim-risk note, status stepper and side state, timeline, schedule (day + time through `content-reschedule`, post IDs and permalinks, trial reels with each version's diff, time, numbers and link, the winner), feedback thread + box, caption (count / 2,200), LinkedIn (the word TasteForm shows as a blue @TasteForm tag; the editor warns when it's missing), sound (bank dropdown + "No music", volume 0–30; a change re-renders), hooks (marked filmed), Jordan's script / visual hook / editing notes / CTA (Edit script opens the editor), the old AI beats read-only, slides, format/funnel/angle/hook style/location/effort, platforms (before scheduling), source idea, footage, results.

### Trial reel? (add-reel sheet and post sheet, before trials start)
`trialChoice`: `on` = trial every version as soon as it's approved, the winner posts to everyone 72 h after the first trial; `off` = post at the slot, no trial; unset = automatic (2+ versions and the slot 3+ days away). Written straight to the row (`trialChoice`, `trialChoiceAt`).

### Winner (trialing reels, and Trial reel: Yes before the trials start)
Calendar / Up next place the reel at the winner's time (`posting.plannedFor`, or projected for Yes: 72 h after the trials start, or Jordan's moved time if 24 h+ after the last trial), with 🧪 markers on trial days. The Winner panel writes `winnerPlan {pick: "v2"|null, platforms: [...], decideNow: true}` straight to the row; the pipeline uses the pick instead of the numbers, posts to those platforms, and with decideNow posts the winner within 25 minutes.

### Statuses
Main: `needs_script → to_film → filmed → editing → edited → awaiting_approval → approved → trialing → scheduled → posted`. Side: `changes_requested` (purple), `needs_jordan` and `error` (red), `skipped`, `cancelled` ("Pulled"). `posting.status`: `trialing | scheduled | posted | error | cancelled`.

## Functions
- `content-update` (deployed v4): `{id, create?, fields}` → admin only, refuses posted posts; creates new rows (`needs_script`, then `to_film` once there's a script), marks the source idea planned, pushes caption / LinkedIn / title changes to a scheduled Outstand post (cancel + re-create; the post id changes), sets `editedByJordanAt`, `scriptChangedAfterEdit`, and `reEditRequested` on a sound change. The repo copy (`supabase/functions/content-update`) also updates a separate YouTube post and keeps `postIds` per platform; **not deployed yet** (the pipeline keeps the YouTube post in line meanwhile).
- `content-reschedule` (deployed v2): `{id, postDate}` → moves a scheduled Outstand post (cancel + re-create) or saves `plannedFor` (unscheduled and trialing rows), then `date, day, weekStart, posting.scheduledFor|plannedFor, posting.movedAt`. Repo copy moves the separate YouTube post too; **not deployed yet**.
- `content-pipeline` (deployed v2): the Mac Mini's data door (merge / get / select on the content tables, read-only `ads_daily` / `ads_campaigns`, `previewUpload` signed URLs for `content-previews`), authenticated with `bundle_api_key()`.
